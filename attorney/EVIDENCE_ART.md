# Evidence artwork

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool. Runtime images are 800 × 600 WebP, bundled in `assets/evidence.js` (about 408 KiB total). Original generated images remain available separately. No generated lettering is used as evidence: verified times and labels are HTML overlays.

## Prompt set
Shared direction: small 4:3 evidence illustrations for Behind the Curtain, refined anime visual novel art, crisp ink contours, softly painted objects, subdued teal shadows, warm brass and cream highlights, readable at 480 pixels, no UI, border, watermark or written text.

1. **Scene report:** heavy brass theatrical mask trophy lying on a dark theatre desk, black rectangular base, snapped curved side handle separately bagged beside a cream police folder. No blood or people.
2. **Microphone slip:** visibly unplugged XLR stage microphone cable beside an audio patch panel, cream maintenance slip with abstract lines and teal pen. Strong readable connector silhouette.
3. **Corridor camera:** empty upstairs backstage corridor, one closed sound-booth door, subdued wall lights and acoustic panels, high-angle security view. No other doors, exits, people or timestamp.
4. **Tessa photograph:** critic at foreground left with black curly puff hair, geometric glasses, coral scarf and blue jacket; large ornate gold mirror at right reflects a director’s desk, a motionless man in white dinner jacket and black bow tie, and the brass award on the floor. Detached handle concealed, no blood or visible injury, no clock or timestamp.
5. **Playback log:** stage-manager console, amber GO button, abstract waveform monitor, sliders, headset and cream cue list. No brands or tiny generated labels.
